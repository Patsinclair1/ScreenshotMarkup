## Description
A small, fast annotator for screenshots. Open a capture, mark it up, and save a copy or copy it straight to the clipboard.

- Freehand pen and a see-through highlighter that keeps text readable on light and dark screens
- Rectangles, ellipses, arrows and straight lines (hold Shift to snap)
- Text boxes you can move, resize and edit until you save
- An eraser that only removes your marks, never the screenshot
- Transcript: reads the text in the image, copies it in one click and makes links clickable
- Zoom from 10% to 800%, pan, undo, and a choice of canvas background

On GNOME, the optional "Screenshot Markup: Edit button" extension adds an **Edit** button to the screenshot notification.
